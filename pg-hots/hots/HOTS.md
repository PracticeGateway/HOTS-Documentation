
HOTS Guide

The following items serve as a checklist to diagnose the most common issues within Practice Gateway. If these steps do not resolve the issue, please escalate the ticket to the Consulting/Development team by assigning it to the Unassigned queue. Be sure to include internal notes detailing the steps taken to identify the issue.
 
Updates to this document will be iterative. Even if you have learned and adapted all the steps listed, please keep this document accessible via IT Glue, as it will be regularly updated with new steps based on ticket resolutions, newly identified issues, and Practice Gateway feature updates.
Data Issues
For many of our clients, the main source of the problem is data issues. When referring to the PMS (Practice Management System), this will be Practice Engine for Scrutton Bland and CCH for other clients.  This information allows you to tell the client which system they should be updating their data in when closing the ticket.

Contact Not Syncing
A contact will only pull through to Practice Gateway if they are connected to a client or are a client in their own right.   You can check this on the clients tab and connections tab in PE.
 
 


They will also not pull through if a client’s email already exists within the system under another account, so check if the email already exists within PG.
